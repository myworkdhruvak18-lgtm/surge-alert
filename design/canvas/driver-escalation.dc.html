After the rider books here
0 to 10 min
Only the blue drivers see the request and can accept or reject it. The rider waits where they are; they walk only after a driver accepts and their details show.
After 10 min
If no one has accepted, the request opens to all nearby drivers, including the green ones inside the hotspot. The ETA shown is the real driver's ETA.
Thin supply
If no blue driver exists nearby, any nearby driver can be booked and the suggestion still shows.
Drivers
Drivers inside the hotspot do not get these rides, but they keep surge-priced rides inside it.
Specification only. The prototype has no drivers; the 500 m and 10 min values are owner-set and need real driver and route data before launch.
